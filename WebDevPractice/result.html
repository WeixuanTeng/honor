15-Min Neighborhood Survey Results

This dashboard summarizes survey responses collected for research on the possibility of a 15-minute life cycle in Seattle. It includes (1) response patterns from the survey, and (2) reference maps showing amenities and a simple “transportation scenario inclusion” model (click to set a starting point and see what amenities are reachable under different travel assumptions).

Click to set a starting point. Scenario rings show reach under different assumptions, and amenity points are highlighted if reachable.
