The 15 Min Neighborhood Survey

This interactive survey map helps us understand how people experience their neighborhood and whether daily needs feel reachable within a 15-minute walk.

If you have a minute, please click the map to mark your neighborhood, answer the questions on the left, and press Send. Thank you for participating!
